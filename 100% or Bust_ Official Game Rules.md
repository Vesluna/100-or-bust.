# **100% or Bust: Official Game Rules**

Welcome to 100% or Bust, a high-stakes conversational deduction game where every word counts and the rules are hidden in plain sight. In this game, one player acts as the Mastermind, setting a conversational minefield, while the other—the Guesser—attempts to navigate it to reach the ultimate goal: a perfect score.

## **The Objective**

The Guesser must reach exactly **100%** by the end of the game. Any final score below 100% results in a loss.

## **Roles**

* **The Mastermind:** Responsible for creating five secret "Penalty Triggers," tracking the Guesser's progress, and calculating the final tally.  
* **The Guesser:** Responsible for sending messages to build their score while attempting to deduce and avoid the hidden penalties.

## **Scoring and Progress**

The game progress is tracked message by message. The Guesser builds their score simply by participating:

* **Base Score:** \+1 point for every message the Guesser sends.  
* **The Tally:** Points are not revealed during the game. The Mastermind keeps a private record and only announces the final percentage at the very end.

## **The Penalty System**

The Mastermind selects five specific conversational rules (e.g., "no emojis," "no messages over 10 words," "no use of the word 'the'"). If the Guesser triggers one of these rules, a penalty is applied to their final score.

| Penalty Level | Point Reduction |
| :---- | :---- |
| Penalty 1 | \-1 Point |
| Penalty 2 | \-2 Points |
| Penalty 3 | \-3 Points |
| Penalty 4 | \-4 Points |
| Penalty 5 | \-5 Points |

*Note: Penalties are cumulative. If a Guesser hits multiple different penalties, all respective points are deducted from the final total.*

## **Game Termination**

The game concludes immediately if either of the following conditions is met:

1. **The Century:** The Guesser reaches their 100th message.  
2. **The Sweep:** The Guesser triggers all five hidden penalties at least once during the game.

## **Winning and Losing**

Once the game ends, the Mastermind calculates the total (Messages Sent minus Penalty Points).

* **Victory:** A final score of exactly 100%.  
* **Defeat:** A final score of 99% or lower.

## **Mastermind Tips**

To keep the game fair but challenging, the Mastermind should choose penalties that cover different aspects of communication:

* **Punctuation:** Using or omitting specific marks.  
* **Word Choice:** Prohibiting common words or requiring specific ones.  
* **Structure:** Message length or formatting (e.g., all lowercase).  
* **Timing:** Replying too quickly or too slowly.
